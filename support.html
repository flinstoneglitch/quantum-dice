How it works

Tap any die to roll it. Each day's rolls are seeded from QeNTROPY, a hybrid entropy engine mixing local device entropy, ANU's quantum random number generator, Amazon Braket, and IBM Quantum hardware when available. You can see today's source and entropy score in the footer.

Common questions

Why did I get haptic feedback / a sound on my roll?
Normal rolls give a light haptic tap. Rolling a natural 20 on the d20 triggers a success haptic and animation; a natural 1 triggers a different pattern. This can't be turned off separately from your device's overall haptics setting.

The share button isn't working.
It opens your device's native share sheet. If nothing appears, try again — if it keeps failing, email us with your device model and iOS version.

I found a bug.
Email colin.damage@gmail.com with what happened and roughly when — a screenshot helps.

Is there an account or sign-in?
No — Quantum Dice has no accounts and doesn't collect any personal data. See the Privacy Policy.
