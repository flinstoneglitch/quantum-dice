🎲 Privacy Policy — Quantum Dice

Last updated September 23, 2026

Quantum Dice ("the app") is a dice-rolling app. This page explains what data it does and doesn't handle.

What we collect

Quantum Dice does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.

What's stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of state locally on your device only, including:

  • Today's daily entropy seed (cached so rolls stay consistent within the day)
  • Your recent roll history, shown in the roll-history list

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Network requests the app makes

To seed each day's rolls, the app requests QeNTROPY (q-runtime.onrender.com) — a public entropy API that returns a random seed value. No personal or device-identifying information is sent with this request.

Native device features

The app uses standard iOS haptic feedback (on rolls) and the system share sheet (to share a result). Both are handled entirely by iOS itself — the app does not receive any data back from these system features beyond what's needed to trigger them.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what data it collects (for example, adding accounts or sync), this page will be updated first.
